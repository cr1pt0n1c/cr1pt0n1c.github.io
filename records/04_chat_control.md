The StB (the Czechoslovak secret police) and every other totalitarian apparatus in history used the exact same rationale. But protecting the vulnerable should never require the preemptive wiretapping of an entire continent.

Criminals are not stupid. Do politicians naively believe that global trafficking rings are going to share their illicit material on a monitored WhatsApp or Messenger chat? We already see human trafficking happening in plain sight—hidden behind innocent-looking listings on platforms like Vinted—and the algorithms miss it entirely.
